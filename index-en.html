Featured Project

mind.set.play | Corporate Game | 2019

Corporate Culture Transformation Game (German language only)

A simulation game for organizational development: As fictional employees, participants work through customer orders while experiencing unexpected events, conflicts, and successes – just like real work life. The game makes modern work approaches and transformation playfully accessible, turning abstract concepts into lived experiences. One game round takes about 90 minutes, accompanied by group coaching. Full workshop: 4-6 hours for 6-18 participants.

The Problem: ▼

A previous attempt to turn VORSPRUNGatwork's consulting approach – based on modern work methods – into an experiential simulation game failed despite five-figure investment and external expertise. The team was skeptical about game-based learning. I got the project without knowing this history.

My Solution: ▼

Iterative development with continuous stakeholder feedback. Focus on simplicity: Role-switching mechanics combined with VUCA event simulation. Players directly experience how personal mindset and team dynamics work under pressure. Ready for deployment with 6-18 participants and facilitator guide.

Result: ▼

Successfully deployed in corporate training sessions. From skeptical team to working product.

Proof: Creative perspective shifts solve problems where conventional approaches fail.

Creative Production Portfolio | Training Projects SRH Heidelberg | 2024-2025

The following projects were created during my training in Game & Multimedia Design at SRH Heidelberg. They demonstrate my range in technical implementation, visual design, and interdisciplinary teamwork – from 3D design through JavaScript development to animation.

Journey |Game Concept & Narrative Design | Unity 3D | Solo | 2025

First-person contemplative experience about rediscovering simple joys of life – a kobold explores five mindfulness stations and finds what truly matters.

Story-driven quest system in fantasy 3D world. Central message: "We already have it and often forget" – reminder of everyday beauty. Five contemplative stations: Nourishment (Apple), Refreshment (Lake), Rest (Meadow), Reflection (Statue), Transition (Portal). 5-10 minute meditative experience.

Handcrafted fantasy aesthetic with emotional warmth. All 3D assets created in Blender – kobold character, environments, interactive objects. Focus on mindfulness, presence, and conscious awareness. Unity implementation with AI-assisted code development. Simple to access, emotionally profound.

Alien foodie Gourmet crash-lands on Earth and must collect delicacies, solve puzzles, and gather energy to fuel his journey home.

Project management and creative collaboration for JavaScript game. Agile team facilitation with self-organizing principles and iterative check-ins to promote team dynamics and goal orientation.

Cross-functional coordination between designers, programmers, and sound designers. Creative collaboration on visual assets and game narrative. Successfully published despite tight deadline.

Chest Quest |Problem-Solving Strategy | Unity 2D | Solo | 2025

Dungeon spirals out of control with wild magic, items falling everywhere – hungry treasure chest seizes the opportunity, devours everything, and accidentally defuses the chaos.

First solo game project with clear challenge: develop Unity prototype without programming knowledge. The solution was a structured AI workflow – from ChatGPT through Claude to today mainly Claude Code.

Developing systematic processes for prompt engineering, memory management, and continuous documentation. In consultation with instructors, transparent attribution standards were established. This approach remains the foundation for professional AI tool use in production and continues to evolve.
